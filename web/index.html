Vector76's strut plate generator, ported to the browser and checked against the original OpenSCAD output at 58 sizes. 1/4" MDF, 1/8" bit (3.5 mm dogbones), 1" EMT wings (11.5 mm). The DXF comes zipped (closed polylines, mm). The G-code follows V1E's milling basics: bolt holes first, then each outline with tabs, 0.25 mm into the spoil board. Check it in a G-code viewer before you cut.

FluidNC config

V1 Engineering's Jackpot3 LowRider config (FluidNC_Configs, JP3_LR, AGPL-3.0, read from GitHub when you click) with the X and Y max_travel_mm set to this cutting area: . Everything else is V1E's (Z travel, homing, pins, motor currents). Or bring your own config.yaml and only its two travel lines change. Check it against your machine before use.


Report a problem (a GitHub issue with your size and file checksums filled in) · Discuss on the V1E forum

The machine is rebuilt from the measured FreeCAD model: every part's position was fitted across 13 FreeCAD builds from 15 x 30 in to 48 x 96 in (3 to 8 braces) and checked part by part against each, and against builds left out of the fit (within 0.1 mm). Fixed for now: 1" EMT (29.5 mm), 6.5 mm XZ plates, the Makita / Kobalt compact router mount. Approximate: Jackpot3 lid, power supply, router colours, the X belt's end loop screws, the vacuum hose route. LowRider 4 parts by V1 Engineering, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0; strut plates by Vector76.
